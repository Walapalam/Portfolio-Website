Here is the activity plan.

The Activity: "The 15-Minute Startup Launch"
The Scenario:

You are the founding team of "DocuSign for Sri Lanka" (A secure, legal digital signature platform for B2B contracts).

The Crisis: A major bank wants to buy your software, but they need a full implementation plan presented in 15 minutes. If you fail, they go to a competitor.

Squad 1: The Architects (Strategy & Requirements)
Focus: Understanding the 'What' and 'Why'.

1. Business Analyst (BA)

Context: The bank wants to know exactly how their loan officers will use this.

Task: Write 3 User Stories for a Loan Officer using the app. (Format: As a [user], I want to [action], so that [benefit]).

Challenge: Don't just say "Sign in." Think about "Batch signing" or "legal verification."

2. Business Development Manager (BDM)

Context: The Bank's CEO is skeptical about the price.

Task: Define the Unique Selling Proposition (USP). Why should they pay for this instead of just emailing PDFs and printing them? (Hint: Security? Speed? Legality?)

3. Business Project Manager

Context: The bank wants this live in 2 weeks. Your team says it takes 6 weeks.

Task: Create a High-Level Roadmap. What Must happen in Week 1 vs Week 2? (Prioritize features: MVP vs. Nice-to-have).

Squad 2: The Builders (Tech & Execution)
Focus: The 'How'.

4. Web Developer

Context: We need a portal that works on the Bank's old desktop computers and the CEO's iPad.

Task: Choose the Tech Stack. (React? Flutter Web? Angular?). Justify your choice based on speed of development vs. performance.

5. Systems Analyst

Context: The new signature system needs to talk to the Bank's ancient internal database.

Task: Draw a simple Data Flow Diagram (DFD). How does data move from the User → Your App → The Bank’s Server?

6. Prompt Engineer

Context: We want an AI chatbot to help users who get stuck signing documents.

Task: Write a System Prompt for the AI.

Constraint: The AI must be helpful but cannot give legal advice. How do you instruct it to refuse legal questions?

Squad 3: The Guardians (Data & Security)
Focus: Risk & Infrastructure.

7. Database Administrator (DBA)

Context: We expect 10,000 signatures a day. We cannot lose a single record.

Task: SQL (Relational) or NoSQL (Document)? Why? Define the Database Schema for a "Contract" (What fields do you need? ID, Timestamp, UserID, Hash?).

8. Security Analyst

Context: If a hacker fakes a signature, the company is dead.

Task: Identify the #1 Vulnerability in a digital signature app (e.g., Identity Theft, Man-in-the-Middle attack) and propose one technical fix (e.g., 2FA, Blockchain hashing).

9. Information Security Officer (ISO)

Context: The Bank asks: "Is this GDPR/PDPA compliant?"

Task: Draft a One-Paragraph Privacy Policy statement. How do you handle user data? Do you delete it after signing?

Squad 4: The Scalers (Growth & Management)
Focus: Delivery & Optimization.

10. Project Manager (Technical)

Context: The Web Developer is sick, and the DBA says the server is crashing. The deadline is tomorrow.

Task: Conducting a Triage Meeting. You have to cut one feature from the launch to save time. Which feature do you kill? (Video verification? Email notifications? Dark mode?)

11. IT Consultant

Context: The Bank asks: "Should we build this signature tool in-house or buy your subscription?"

Task: Do a quick Build vs. Buy Analysis. Give 2 reasons why "Buying" your tool is better than them hiring 10 devs to build it.

12. Digital Marketing Analyst

Context: We need the Bank employees to actually use the tool once it's bought.

Task: Define 3 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) to track success. (e.g., Adoption Rate, Time-to-Sign, Customer Support Tickets).

Huddle: Tell them to find their "Squad Members" (Squad 1, 2, 3, 4).

The Timer: Give them strictly 10-15 Minutes. (Create pressure—that's the real world).

The Pitch: Pick one representative from each Squad to present their solution in 60 seconds.

This format proves to them that BIS is not just one job—it is an ecosystem where every role relies on the other.
