TEAM 1: THE REQUIREMENTS CLASH

SCENARIO: “The University Event App”

THE SITUATION: The Student Union wants a “Voting App” for the next election.

  • The Students want: “Anonymous voting” and “Live real-time results.”
  • The Administration wants: “Verified Student IDs only” (No anonymous) and “Results hidden until approval.”

YOUR MISSION: You are the Product Team. You cannot satisfy everyone perfectly. You must find a compromise.

DISCUSS & SOLVE:

  1. The Conflict: If you make it “Verified ID”, students might not trust it. If you make it “Anonymous”, people might vote twice. How do you solve this?
  2. The Feature: Design the “Login Screen”. What does it say to reassure students their vote is secret?
  3. The Pitch: How do you sell this solution to the angry Student Union President?

OUTPUT: Present your Compromise Solution that keeps both sides (mostly) happy.
