TEAM 6: THE SECURITY BALANCE

SCENARIO: “Safety vs. Speed”

THE SITUATION: Your company handles sensitive medical data.

  • The Security Team wants: “12-character passwords + Special Characters + 2-Factor Authentication (SMS & Email) + Change password every 30 days.”
  • The Doctors (Users) say: “I don’t have time for this! I have patients dying! If I can’t login in 5 seconds, I’m writing passwords on a sticky note.”

YOUR MISSION: You are the Security Consultants. If you make it too safe, they bypass it (Sticky Notes). If you make it too easy, you get hacked.

DISCUSS & SOLVE:

  1. The Threat: Why is the “Sticky Note” actually the biggest security risk here?
  2. The Balance: Which security rule can you relax? (Maybe remove the “Change every 30 days” rule? Or use Fingerprint instead of Password?)
  3. The Pitch: Create a policy that is Secure enough but Fast enough.

OUTPUT: Propose your New Login Policy for the doctors.
