# CHEAT SHEET: Role Responsibilities

Use this sheet to help you act out your role in the Team Crisis Activity.

## STRATEGY SQUAD (Team 1 & General BAs)

- **Business Analyst (BA):**
  - **Main Job:** Translate "Business Want" into "Tech Need".
  - **Refrain:** "I hear what you want, but here is what you actually need..."
  - **Focus:** Requirements, Features, User Stories.
- **Project Manager (PM):**
  - **Main Job:** Manage Time, Budget, and Scope. The "No" person.
  - **Refrain:** "That is out of scope." or "We don't have budget for that."
  - **Focus:** Timelines, Deadlines, Prioritization.

## TECH SQUAD (Team 2 & General Devs)

- **Tech Lead:**
  - **Main Job:** Make the final technical decision.
  - **Refrain:** "Let's re-architect this." or "It's a feature, not a bug."
  - **Focus:** Architecture, Scalability, Code Quality.
- **Systems Analyst:**
  - **Main Job:** Figure out how System A talks to System B.
  - **Refrain:** "The API documentation is missing."
  - **Focus:** Integrations, Data Flow, Legacy Systems.

## RISK SQUAD (Team 3 & Security/Data)

- **Security Analyst / CISO:**
  - **Main Job:** Protect the system from bad actors (and internal mistakes).
  - **Refrain:** "That's a security risk."
  - **Focus:** Vulnerabilities, Hacking, Compliance (GDPR).
- **Compliance/Legal Officer:**
  - **Main Job:** Keep the company from getting sued.
  - **Refrain:** "Is this GDPR compliant?"
  - **Focus:** Law, Privacy, Contracts.

## GROWTH SQUAD (Team 4 & Product)

- **Product Manager:**
  - **Main Job:** Decide "What" we actully build for the user.
  - **Refrain:** "This is the MVP (Minimum Viable Product)."
  - **Focus:** User Experience, Market Fit, Features.
- **Marketing/Data Analyst:**
  - **Main Job:** Get users and prove success with numbers.
  - **Refrain:** "What is the ROI (Return on Investment)?"
  - **Focus:** Growth, Metrics, Analytics.
