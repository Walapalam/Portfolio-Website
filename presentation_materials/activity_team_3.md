# TEAM 3: THE DESIGN CONFLICT

**SCENARIO: "Marketing vs. Users"**

**THE SITUATION:**
You are redesigning the Homepage.

- **The Marketing Director:** Wants a _Giant Pop-up_ asking for emails as soon as the site loads. ("We need leads!")
- **The UX Designer:** Says Pop-ups are annoying and make users leave. ("It ruins the experience!")

**YOUR MISSION:**
You are the Product Owners. Both sides have a point (Leads = Money, UX = Retention).
You need a middle ground.

**DISCUSS & SOLVE:**

1.  **The Compromise:** Can you get emails _without_ an annoying pop-up? (Where else can you put the form?)
2.  **The Logic:** If you MUST use a pop-up, _when_ should it appear? (Immediately? After scrolling? On exit?)
3.  **The Sketch:** Describe how the new page layout balances "Sales" vs "Usability".

**OUTPUT:**
Describe your **Design Solution** that makes Marketing happy but doesn't anger Users.
