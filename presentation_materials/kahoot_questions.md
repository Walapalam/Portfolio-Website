# Kahoot Question Bank: The BIS Edition

## Question 1: The Requirement Paradox

**Question:** The client says "I want the system to be 'Easy to use'". As a BA, what is the problem?

- A) It's a great requirement!
- B) "Easy" is subjective and not measurable
- C) Developers love vague requirements
- D) It's too specific
  **Correct Answer:** B ("Easy" means nothing. "3 clicks to checkout" means something.)

## Question 2: Scope Creep

**Question:** It is 2 days before deadline. The client asks for a "Small feature: A Chatbot". What is this called?

- A) Agile Development
- B) A friendly request
- C) Scope Creep
- D) Innovation
  **Correct Answer:** C (The enemy of all projects!)

## Question 3: The Gap Analysis

**Question:** What is a "Gap Analysis"?

- A) Finding the gap in the fence
- B) Comparing "Current State" vs "Desired Future State"
- C) Analyzing why the code is broken
- D) Testing the gap between buttons
  **Correct Answer:** B

## Question 4: Stakeholder Management

**Question:** The CEO wants X. The Users want Y. The Devs want Z. Who do you listen to?

- A) Only the CEO (He pays)
- B) Only the Devs (They build)
- C) You facilitate a workshop to find the "Business Value" compromise
- D) You quit
  **Correct Answer:** C (Welcome to the life of a BA/PM)

## Question 5: Legacy Systems

**Question:** Why do banks still use 30-year-old systems?

- A) They like vintage stuff
- B) It is too risky and expensive to replace them
- C) They forgot the password
- D) No one knows how to code anymore
  **Correct Answer:** B (Risk mitigation is key in BIS)

## Question 6: UAT (User Acceptance Testing)

**Question:** When does UAT happen?

- A) Before coding starts
- B) After the internal testing, before Go-Live
- C) Never
- D) Whenever the user feels like it
  **Correct Answer:** B

## Question 7: The Agile Myth

**Question:** Does "Agile" mean "No Documentation"?

- A) Yes, write nothing!
- B) No, it means "Just Enough Documentation"
- C) Agile is just a buzzword
- D) It means we change requirements every hour
  **Correct Answer:** B

## Question 8: The Developer vs BA

**Question:** A Developer tells you "This feature is impossible." What do you do?

- A) Believe them immediately
- B) Ask: "Is it impossible, or just really hard/expensive?"
- C) Fire them
- D) Learn to code yourself
  **Correct Answer:** B (Usually it's just hard, not impossible. Your job is to check the ROI.)

## Question 9: GDPR

**Question:** You are storing User Emails. What must you ensure?

- A) That you sell them to advertisers
- B) That you have Consent and a Privacy Policy
- C) That you print them out
- D) Use them for your newsletter without asking
  **Correct Answer:** B

## Question 10: The MVP

**Question:** What does MVP stand for in Startup/Agile terms?

- A) Most Valuable Player
- B) Minimum Viable Product (Smallest thing that works)
- C) Maximum Viable Product (Everything included)
- D) Most Viable Person
  **Correct Answer:** B
