TEAM 5: THE TEAM CONFLICT

SCENARIO: “The Feature Refusal”

THE SITUATION:

  • The Business Analyst (BA) wrote a requirement: “The search bar must autocomplete names instantly.”
  • The Senior Developer replies: “That will make the database crash. I’m not doing it. It’s stupid.” They are now arguing in the Slack channel.

YOUR MISSION: You are the Project Managers. You need to de-escalate this. The BA is technically right (users want it). The Dev is technically right (it’s risky).

DISCUSS & SOLVE:

  1. The De-escalation: Who do you talk to first?
  2. The Technical Compromise: Is there a way to have autocomplete without crashing? (Maybe wait until 3 letters are typed?)
  3. The Culture: How do you stop them from fighting in public Slack channels?

OUTPUT: What is your Resolution Plan? How do you get the feature built without breaking the team?
