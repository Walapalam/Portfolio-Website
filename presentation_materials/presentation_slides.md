## Slide 13: Role Cheat Sheet 1 (Strategy & Tech)

_(Project this during discussion)_

### **BUSINESS ROLES**

- **Business Analyst (BA):** Translates "Business Want" -> "Tech Need".  
  _Task:_ Find the compromise between stakeholders.
- **Project Manager (PM):** Manages Priorities.  
  _Task:_ "Can we do this cheaper/faster?"

### **TECH ROLES**

- **Tech Lead:** Evaluates Risk and Feasibility.  
  _Task:_ "Is this code actually buildable?"
- **Security Analyst:** Evaluates Safety.  
  _Task:_ "Where is the vulnerability?"

## Slide 14: Role Cheat Sheet 2 (Data & Product)

### **DATA & DESIGN**
