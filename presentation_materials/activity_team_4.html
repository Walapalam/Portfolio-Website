TEAM 4: THE DATA DEFINITION

SCENARIO: “What is an Active User?”

THE SITUATION: The CEO wants a report on “Active Users” for the investor meeting.

  • The Developer counts an “Active User” as: Anyone who logged in this month. (Count: 5,000)
  • The Sales Team counts an “Active User” as: Anyone who bought something this month. (Count: 500)
  • The Investor counts an “Active User” as: Anyone who visited the site (even without logging in). (Count: 50,000)

YOUR MISSION: You are the Data Analysts. If you pick the wrong number, you are either lying or underselling.

DISCUSS & SOLVE:

  1. The Definition: Which definition is the most “Honest” for a growing startup?
  2. The Context: Why does the Developer’s number matter? Why does the Sales number matter?
  3. The Report: How do you present this to the CEO so he understands the difference?

OUTPUT: Define what “Active User” means for this company and why.
