Kahoot Question Bank: The BIS Edition

Question 1: The Requirement Paradox

Question: The client says “I want the system to be ‘Easy to use’”. As a BA, what is the problem?

    1. It’s a great requirement!
    1. “Easy” is subjective and not measurable
    1. Developers love vague requirements
    1. It’s too specific Correct Answer: B (“Easy” means nothing. “3 clicks to checkout” means something.)

Question 2: Scope Creep

Question: It is 2 days before deadline. The client asks for a “Small feature: A Chatbot”. What is this called?

    1. Agile Development
    1. A friendly request
    1. Scope Creep
    1. Innovation Correct Answer: C (The enemy of all projects!)

Question 3: The Gap Analysis

Question: What is a “Gap Analysis”?

    1. Finding the gap in the fence
    1. Comparing “Current State” vs “Desired Future State”
    1. Analyzing why the code is broken
    1. Testing the gap between buttons Correct Answer: B

Question 4: Stakeholder Management

Question: The CEO wants X. The Users want Y. The Devs want Z. Who do you listen to?

    1. Only the CEO (He pays)
    1. Only the Devs (They build)
    1. You facilitate a workshop to find the “Business Value” compromise
    1. You quit Correct Answer: C (Welcome to the life of a BA/PM)

Question 5: Legacy Systems

Question: Why do banks still use 30-year-old systems?

    1. They like vintage stuff
    1. It is too risky and expensive to replace them
    1. They forgot the password
    1. No one knows how to code anymore Correct Answer: B (Risk mitigation is key in BIS)

Question 6: UAT (User Acceptance Testing)

Question: When does UAT happen?

    1. Before coding starts
    1. After the internal testing, before Go-Live
    1. Never
    1. Whenever the user feels like it Correct Answer: B

Question 7: The Agile Myth

Question: Does “Agile” mean “No Documentation”?

    1. Yes, write nothing!
    1. No, it means “Just Enough Documentation”
    1. Agile is just a buzzword
    1. It means we change requirements every hour Correct Answer: B

Question 8: The Developer vs BA

Question: A Developer tells you “This feature is impossible.” What do you do?

    1. Believe them immediately
    1. Ask: “Is it impossible, or just really hard/expensive?”
    1. Fire them
    1. Learn to code yourself Correct Answer: B (Usually it’s just hard, not impossible. Your job is to check the ROI.)

Question 9: GDPR

Question: You are storing User Emails. What must you ensure?

    1. That you sell them to advertisers
    1. That you have Consent and a Privacy Policy
    1. That you print them out
    1. Use them for your newsletter without asking Correct Answer: B

Question 10: The MVP

Question: What does MVP stand for in Startup/Agile terms?

    1. Most Valuable Player
    1. Minimum Viable Product (Smallest thing that works)
    1. Maximum Viable Product (Everything included)
    1. Most Viable Person Correct Answer: B
