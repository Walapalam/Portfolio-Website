TEAM 2: THE TECH DEBATE

SCENARIO: “Buy vs. Build”

THE SITUATION: Your company needs a “Ticketing Support System” (like Jira).

  • Option A (Buy): Pay $5,000/month for “Zendesk”. It works immediately, but you can’t customize it much.
  • Option B (Build): The Dev Team says “We can build our own for free in 2 months!” (It will be exactly what we want).

YOUR MISSION: You are the Tech Consultants. You know that “2 months” usually means “6 months”. But $5,000/month is a lot of money.

DISCUSS & SOLVE:

  1. The Analysis: List 2 Risks of Building it yourselves. (Maintenance? Bugs?)
  2. The Analysis: List 2 Risks of Buying it. (Cost? Data dependency?)
  3. The Verdict: Which one do you choose for a Startup vs a Big Bank?

OUTPUT: Give us your final recommendation: Buy or Build? And One Reason Why.
