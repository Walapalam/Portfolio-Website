The Pivot: 24/7 vs. 9-5

  • The Traditional Path: Apply -> Wait -> 9-5 Job.
  • The Pivot Option: Startups, Accelerators, & Freelancing.
  • Why Pivot?
    • Accelerated Growth: You aren't just an intern; you are a builder.
    • Higher Stakes: Production-ready code vs. "University Projects".
  • The Takeaway: You don't have to settle for a standard role. If you understand both Code and Business, you can carve your own path.

Role Cheat Sheet: All Squads

STRATEGY SQUAD

Business Analyst (BA)
Job: Translate "Business Want" to "Tech Need".
Refrain: "Here is what you actually need..."

Project Manager (PM)
Job: Manage Time, Budget, Scope.
Focus: "No, we don't have budget."

TECH SQUAD

Tech Lead
Job: Final technical decision.
Refrain: "It's a feature, not a bug."

Systems Analyst
Job: How System A talks to System B.
Focus: Integrations & APIs.

RISK SQUAD

Security Analyst
Job: Protect from bad actors.
Refrain: "That's a security risk."

Compliance Officer
Job: Keep us from getting sued.
Focus: GDPR & Legal.

GROWTH SQUAD

Product Manager
Job: Decide "What" to build (MVP).
Focus: User Experience.

Marketing Analyst
Job: Get users, prove ROI.
Refrain: "What do the numbers say?"
